# Public examples worth imitating

He asks for concrete examples he can copy, not frameworks. These are the ones that were shared
and used. Check a link still works before sending it.

## Direction docs

| Example | Borrow |
|---|---|
| [Tesla, Master Plan, Part Deux](https://www.tesla.com/master-plan-part-deux) and the [2006 plan](https://www.tesla.com/secret-master-plan) | The whole shape: recap, purpose, a few moves, "So, in short". Tesla's site refuses automated reads, so ask him to paste the text if exact wording matters. |
| [Slack, "We Don't Sell Saddles Here"](https://medium.com/@stewart/we-dont-sell-saddles-here-4c59524d650d) | The "we sell X, not software" thesis, in one person's voice. |
| Amazon's working-backwards press release: [the format](https://medium.com/intrico-io/strategy-tool-amazons-pr-faq-72b3e49aa167), [a worked example](https://slab.com/library/examples/amazon-working-backwards-press-release) | The FAQ of hard questions, answered up front. |
| Airbnb, Project Snow White: [Sequoia](https://articles.sequoiacap.com/visualizing-customer-experience), [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/1681924/how-snow-white-helped-airbnbs-mobile-mission) | The customer's journey frame by frame, and the gaps it exposes. |
| GitLab's public direction pages ([archived Team Planning page](https://web.archive.org/web/20250121224422/https://about.gitlab.com/direction/plan/project_management/team_planning/)) | A per-area direction page with explicit boundaries. |
| [Linear Method](https://linear.app/method) | Beliefs as one opinion and one sentence of why. |

## Ways to shape a short story

| Shape | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| And, But, Therefore | [The ABT framework](https://www.aaas.org/events/aristotle-south-park-abt-framework-new-tool-science-communication) | Saying it in the room in three sentences. |
| The Story Spine | [NPR interview with Kenn Adams](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5750619) | Telling it as the user's story. |
| What is, what could be | [Nancy Duarte's write-up](https://www.duarte.com/resources/secret-structure-great-talks/) | Today and next year in pairs. The moves already do this. |
| The strategic narrative | [Andy Raskin with Lenny](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-power-of-strategic-narrative) | Naming the change in the world first. |
| Situation, Complication, Question, Answer | [A short guide to the Minto approach](https://www.monash.edu/student-academic-success/excel-at-writing/how-to-write/business-paper-using-the-minto-approach) | The top half of the page already follows it. |
| Diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions | Richard Rumelt, "Good Strategy Bad Strategy" | Testing that the moves follow from the diagnosis. |

## Visual-first templates for one slide

| Example | Template |
|---|---|
| [Disney's 1957 strategy chart](https://kottke.org/15/06/walt-disneys-corporate-strategy-chart) | One asset in the centre, every business around it, arrows that say what each gives the other. |
| [Steve Jobs's four-box grid](https://swipefile.com/steve-jobs-simple-product-matrix) | Two rows, two columns, one thing per box. |
| [Apple's keynote "bento" slides](https://www.macstories.net/news/wwdc-2025-all-the-small-things-bento-box-version/) | One big tile for the headline, small tiles for the rest. |

He tried the first and preferred plain rows. Offer these as options, build one only when asked.

## Language

The NFX talk "Magic Words" (Speed Masterclass, episode 5): language first, product second, and
say what the thing is to the user. It is the test behind the headline rules in `SKILL.md`.
